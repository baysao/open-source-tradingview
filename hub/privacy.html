Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-25. Plain language, no legalese: what this site holds, for how long, and who else touches it.

What we hold

When you sign in with Google, the site stores the minimum it needs to recognise your account next time:

  • your Google account id, your email address and your display name;
  • sign-in session records (so you stay signed in), including a creation time and an expiry;
  • nothing else about you. There is no analytics profile, no advertising identifier and no tracking.

The charts' load-time statistic (time to first candle) is stored as a count in fixed buckets with no identity attached to it: no account, no IP address, no session. It cannot be linked back to you.

While you use the charts, the site talks to the market-data providers below, but it does not send them your account data.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days since your last use (a sliding window), then deleted.
  • Saved layouts and settings: backed up daily, each backup kept 30 days.
  • Server backups: kept 14 days, then overwritten.

Who else touches your data

  • Google: the sign-in provider. You sign in with your Google account; Google's own privacy policy covers that step.
  • Cloudflare: carries traffic to this site through its tunnel. Cloudflare sees the connection like any network operator.
  • Deriv, Bybit and Binance: the market-data sources. The site requests public chart data from them; your requests go through this server, so they see the server, not you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the account menu in the header, without contacting anyone. Deleting removes your account row, and the database's foreign-key cascade removes everything attached to it in the same step: your Google sign-in link, your sessions, and your saved desktop sign-in codes. After the next backup rotation, the deleted data leaves the backups too.
