Delete your TradeX account?

This deletes, from the server:

  • your account: name, email, avatar and the Google sign-in link
  • every signed-in session, on all devices
  • saved layouts, drawings, Pine scripts, favourites, theme and their backups
  • your MT5 bridges and their tokens

This cannot be undone. What is stored in this browser stays here, and you can keep using TradeX signed out.
