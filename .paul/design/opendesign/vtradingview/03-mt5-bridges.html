Settings

Add an MT5 bridge

Error: address refused. 10.0.0.5 is a private address. Publish the bridge on a public HTTPS name, for example through a Cloudflare Tunnel.
Error: certificate not valid for mt5.example.net. Renew it or fix the name, then test again.
Error: wrong token. The bridge answered 401. Copy BRIDGE_TOKEN from the bridge's .env again.
Error: timed out after 10 s. Check that the bridge and its tunnel are running.
