Privacy

How TradeX on vtradingview.com handles your data.

Draft for the site owner: confirm each statement against the deployed server before the public launch, and add the contact address.

Without an account

You can use every chart without signing in. Your drawings, scripts, favourites and theme stay in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to the server.

With a Google account

When you sign in with Google, we store:

Account
Your email, name, avatar URL and Google account id (sub). Nothing else from Google.
Sessions
A session record per signed-in device, so you stay signed in. Signing out ends it.
Saved state
Layouts, drawings, Pine scripts, favourites and theme, so they follow you to other devices. Backups made at sign-in are deleted after 30 days. Nightly server backups are kept for 14 days.
MT5 bridges
The bridge URL and label you enter, and its token encrypted on the server.

Logs and measurements

  • Server logs keep recent lines in memory with tokens and emails redacted.
  • Chart load time is measured in milliseconds with the chart engine name, and no user id.

Deleting your data

Choose Delete account in the avatar menu. The server deletes your account, every session, your saved state and backups, and your MT5 bridges at once. It cannot be undone. Data in your browser stays until you clear it.

Contact

Questions about your data: contact address to be added by the site owner.
